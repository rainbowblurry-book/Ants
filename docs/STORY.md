# Story — "ANTS!"

## Premise
The **Blue Caps** are a rowdy little colony living under a giant jungle leaf. One day humans arrive, clear the jungle, and a gardener scoops up the Blue Caps' home flower, **Queen Marigold** included, into a flower pot. The pot is carried from clearing to camp to village to house, and finally to a sunny kitchen windowsill. The Blue Caps march after their Queen through the human world. Every new place is already claimed by a rival ant colony that wants the same crumbs.

The humans never notice any of it. They are giant, clumsy and oblivious, and they are part of the scenery, never the enemy (CREATIVE §1). The Blue Caps reach the kitchen and find the Queen happily installed in her pot with a view of the old jungle. They win the windowsill from the Crumb Crown and throw a party.

## Recurring cast (player colony, blue + circle)
- **Captain Pip:** the cocky leader. Always grinning, always "I meant to do that."
- **Dot:** a sharp-eyed aimer who squints at everything, even soup. Gives aiming tips in the tutorial.
- **Bruno:** big, strong, and constantly saying "oops". The comic relief.
- **Queen Marigold:** cheerful and unbothered. She sends postcards (crumb notes) from each stop; these become the comic hooks.

Rivals always use the amber team with a rounded-triangle badge (DR-003). Each colony adds a signature accessory, and each boss is 1.6× scale with a unique headpiece (CREATIVE §2).

## Campaign structure (DR-008)
- 10 biomes × 10 levels. Within each biome:
  - L1–3 **teach** the biome's new terrain or wind idea.
  - L4–6 **test** it against tougher rivals.
  - L7–8 **twist** it (combine it with an earlier biome's idea).
  - L9 **rest**: a relaxed, fun level with lots of loot.
  - L10 **boss**.
- Every boss has high HP, a terrain advantage (a fortress), wind manipulation, and cluster ammo. **Every boss trick is telegraphed one turn ahead.**
- Exact numbers (HP, wind ranges, AI curve) go in PLAN.md with Decision IDs.
